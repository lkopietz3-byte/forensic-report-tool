# Outreach and sourcing

This repository contains product research examples. It does not document sent messages, confirmed design partners, or a live offer. Before contacting anyone, verify the recipient, the current product behavior, the links, and any proposed terms.

Keep real names, contact details, referrals, replies, and interview notes in private, access-controlled storage outside this repository. The committed `contacts.csv` is a header-only example; do not populate or commit it.

When describing the tool, say that it organizes expert-supplied material into a report draft and checks whether citation IDs refer to supplied evidence. That check does not establish factual support. The expert must review the sources, facts, citations, opinions, and final report. Do not promise admissibility, legal compliance, complete AI-use logging, or a particular confidentiality property without current evidence and appropriate review.
