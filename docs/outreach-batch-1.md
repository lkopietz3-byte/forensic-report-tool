# Sample outreach language

These are unsent examples, not a record of contacted experts, confirmed customer demand, or an active offer. Replace placeholders with verified details before use. Keep real names, replies, and interview notes outside this public repository in private, access-controlled storage.

## Research interview request

> Hi [Name] — I am learning how [discipline] experts assemble reports from their own findings. Would you be willing to discuss your workflow? I am interested in where a report-organizing tool might help and where it would create risk.

## Product feedback request

> Hi [Name] — I am testing a tool that organizes expert-supplied material into a report draft and records logged AI use. It checks whether cited evidence IDs were supplied, but it cannot verify that a source supports a sentence. Experts must review every fact, citation, opinion, and final report. If you would like to see a sample, I can share one after confirming the link and current product behavior.

Neither example promises legal compliance, admissibility, confidentiality, complete logging, or commercial terms. Those statements require separate current evidence and review.
