# First Outreach Batch — ready to send (once the site is live)

Concrete, human-sounding messages for your first ~10 contacts. They are written to
sound like *you typed them*, not like a template. **Before sending each one:**
1. Swap in the real name and a **real, specific first line** (the bracketed hook is
   an example — replace it with something you actually saw about them).
2. Send from your personal address, not a no-reply.
3. Read it out loud. If a sentence sounds like marketing, cut it.
4. Confirm the links work on the live domain (`/sample`, `/for-experts`).

Honesty bar (same as the product): *structures your own findings · restricts the
model to supplied evidence · checks missing and unknown citation IDs · you verify,
prepare, and sign · admissibility is the court's call.*
Never write "court-defensible," "admissible," or "guaranteed."

## 1 · Vocational-rehab expert — cold email (the time sink)
*Hook: their directory/IARP listing, a case they're named in, a CV detail.*

**Subject:** the records-reviewed section you'd rather not write

> low-stakes question for a working expert.
>
> I'm building a tool that does the report-writing slog without putting words in
> your mouth: you give it your findings, it lays them out in Rule 26 format, and it

> was used — handy now that "did you use AI?" is becoming a deposition question.
>
> There's nothing to buy. I just want a few real experts to tell me where it's
> wrong. Two-minute sample: [link]/sample. If it's worth your time, the short
> version of "what's missing" is here: [link]/for-experts.
>
> Either way — appreciate you reading this far.
> [Your name]

### A few rules that make cold outreach work
- **One specific, true detail** about them beats any amount of polish. If you can't
  find one, you're emailing the wrong person or haven't looked hard enough.
- **Lead with their problem, not your product.** The product is the second half of
  the email, at most.
- **Make the ask tiny:** "tell me what's wrong with this," not "hop on a call."
- **Volume + patience:** 10–15 a week, two follow-ups max, then let it go. Two good
  design partners is the whole goal — you don't need a hundred replies.
