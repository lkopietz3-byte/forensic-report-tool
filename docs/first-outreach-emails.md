# Example first-contact emails

These are unsent sample messages. They do not show a verified customer relationship, design-partner program, price, or delivered service. Confirm every recipient detail, link, and product claim before adapting them. Store actual contact information and replies privately, outside this public repository.

## Ask about the workflow

**Subject:** How do you assemble your expert reports?

> Hi [Name] — I am researching how [discipline] experts turn their findings and source material into a final report. Would you be open to a short conversation about the steps that take the most time and the parts you would not delegate to software?

## Ask for feedback on a sample

**Subject:** Feedback on a report-organizing sample

> Hi [Name] — I am testing a tool that organizes an expert's own supplied material into a report draft and records logged AI use. Its citation check confirms that referenced IDs were supplied; it does not establish factual support. The expert must review and sign the final report. If you are interested, I can send a sample after verifying the current link and behavior.

Neither message claims that the tool verifies facts, produces admissible reports, captures every AI action, or is suitable for confidential case material without a separate deployment and data-handling review.
